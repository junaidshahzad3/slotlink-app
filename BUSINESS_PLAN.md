## 1. Executive Summary & Thesis Upgrade

### Why v1.0 Needed a Rethink
SlotLink v1.0 was positioned as a **"1-Link Micro-Portal"** ($12–$19/mo) competing primarily on simplicity against Calendly and bloated CRMs (TutorBird, HoneyBook). Upon deep scrutiny, three structural bottlenecks limited v1.0:

1. **Commodity Perception:** Solo tutors view a standalone calendar link as an interchangeable cost center (free on Calendly) rather than an indispensable profit driver.
2. **The "Payer ≠ User" Blind Spot:** In K–12 test prep, STEM tutoring, and music coaching, the **student** attends lessons and uses study sheets, while the **parent** pays the `$250–$600` multi-hour pack invoice. When a balance hits `1 hour left`, parents hesitate to renew without quantified proof of score trajectory and mastery.
3. **Flat-Rate Revenue Ceiling:** At `$12/mo` flat MRR, scaling to `$10,000/month` requires ~833 active paying tutors with zero expansion revenue when those tutors scale from `$1,500/mo` to `$8,000/mo` in pack sales.

### The SlotLink 2.0 Thesis
SlotLink 2.0 makes money **for** the educator across every stage of the student lifecycle:
- **Acquire (`/[slug]`):** A **60-Second Interactive Diagnostic & Score Predictor** turns passive link clicks into warm diagnostic leads pre-attached to 5-Hour Packs.
- **Prove & Renew (`/p/[token]`):** A zero-login **Parent ROI & Score Trajectory Dossier** visualizes verified progress (`580 Baseline → 710 Current → 760 Goal`, `92% Drill Accuracy`) right beside a **1-Tap Instant Pack Renewal** button.
- **Automate (`/dashboard`):** An **Autonomous Renewal Autopilot** detects low balances (`<= 2 hrs`), drafts copy-ready WhatsApp/SMS/email **Parent ROI Digests**, and tracks score-linked renewals cleanly.

## 3. Target Customer Segments & "Payer ≠ User" Psychology

Primary Beachhead Markets: **US, UK, Canada, Europe, and Australia** (`$50–$120/hr` solo practitioners).

1. **High-Stakes Exam Prep Tutors (Tier-1 Wedge):**
   - *Niches:* Digital SAT, ACT, AP Calculus/Physics, UCAT/BMAT (UK), 11+ Grammar Prep (UK), NAPLAN/ATAR (AU).
   - *Buyer Psychology:* Parents willingly spend `$250–$950` per pack when they see structured diagnostics (`+120 pt` projected gain) and transparent session-by-session score progress rather than vague "we practiced math today" texts.
2. **Executive & Adult Fluency Coaches:**
   - *Niches:* GMAT/GRE quant, IELTS/TOEFL business fluency, technical interview prep.
   - *Buyer Psychology:* Busy professionals refuse to create accounts or remember passwords; `.ics` calendar drops and zero-login magic links maximize repeat pack velocity.
3. **Conservatory Music & Skill Coaches:**
   - *Niches:* Piano, violin, vocal audition coaching, chess coaching.
   - *Buyer Psychology:* Automated Google Calendar conflict guards + 24h reschedule protection cut unpaid last-minute cancellations while study-sheet drills build weekly accountability.
